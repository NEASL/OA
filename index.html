What they judge: C · A · R

The company uses the same three criteria at every stage, online and in person. Official

C

Capacity

Can you think it through?

Learn fast, analyse information, solve problems, suggest ideas.

In sales: find what the customer really needs, and use the numbers.

A

Achievement

Do you get things done?

Drive, resilience, confidence, and delivering results.

In sales: hit a target, and keep going after a no.

R

Relationships

Do people like working with you?

Teamwork, clear communication, respect for different people.

In sales: build trust, and influence people who are not your team.

Their stated values are honesty, integrity and respect. Many work-situation questions quietly test exactly these three. Tip

Station 1 of 3

Realistic job preview

Optional · about 5 minutes · at the start of the tryout

What it is

Pages and short videos about the job and the programme. They come before the job tryout. Official

Why they use it

To show you the real work, so you can decide if it fits you. It probably does not count towards your score.

Try it

Use it as a free hint sheet.

  1. Read it once, calmly.
  2. Write down three to five words or values that keep coming back.
  3. Use them later, honestly, in the work situations and in your answer to why you want to join.

Learn

What a company repeats about itself is usually what it rewards. Tip

Station 2 of 3

Virtual Job Tryout

About 30 minutes · four parts, A to D · no camera

What it is

A job simulation. You handle work challenges, show how you solve problems, tell your story and describe how you like to work. You can save and come back, as long as you finish within the 7 days. Official

Why they use it

It shows your judgement, your background and your work style in one go, for thousands of applicants. The system gives a fit score for each skill.

Before you start

  • Use a laptop if you can. There is some reading.
  • Read every instruction screen. The format changes between parts.
  • Treat each answer as final. Some versions do not let you go back.
  • The system can move people forward automatically above a set score. Take every part seriously. Official

Part A · about 8 situations · 8–12 min (estimate)

Work challenges

What it is

Short work situations. You choose the best response from a few options. Candidates report four options per question. Reported

Why they use it

To see your judgement when the right answer is not obvious: honesty, ownership, safety and care for people.

Try it

A client calls. Their delivery is two days late and they are angry. Your manager is away. What do you do?

Tap the option you would choose.

Learn: the pattern of strong answers

  1. You act yourself. You do not wait or pass it on.
  2. You tell the truth. No promises you cannot keep.
  3. You protect people, data and safety first.
  4. You bring in the right person when it is their decision.
  5. You choose the calm, fair option over the dramatic one.

Seen before

  • A colleague did something wrong. How do you address it? Four options.Reported · Nov 2025
  • A colleague asks you to send project files to their private email. What do you do?Reported · 2026
  • Questions about workplace ethics and doing the right thing.Reported · Nov 2025

Tip for the private email case

Keep company data in company channels. Say no kindly, and offer a safe way to share.

Part B · about 8 situations · 8–12 min (estimate)

Working with colleagues

What it is

Team situations. Some versions give you a short email, chat or table to read first. Unconfirmed

Why they use it

To see how you work with others, and whether you use information carefully.

Try it

Email · from Sam, team lead

The client meeting moved to Thursday 10:00. Slides are due Wednesday 17:00. Noor does the numbers, you do the story.

Chat · Noor, Tuesday

I can't finish the numbers before Thursday morning.

What do you do first?

Read the email and the chat, then tap the option you would choose.

Learn

  • Read everything first. Check names, dates and numbers.
  • Pick the answer that solves the problem and keeps people working together.
  • Go to the manager when the team cannot fix it, or when it is their call.

Seen before

  • A prep seller lists about eight teamwork questions that refer to short documents.Unconfirmed · 2025/26
  • Candidates describe the tryout as workplace situations plus questions about you.Reported · 2026

Tip

The trap in these is usually a detail: a deadline, a name or a number.

Part C · about 13 questions · 2–5 min (estimate)

Your story

What it is

Multiple-choice questions about your past: study, jobs, activities, results, and how others see you. Official

Why they use it

Your past is a good guide to how you will work. They look for drive and a real track record.

Try it

Compared with other students, how often did you take the lead in group projects?

There is no correct option here. Tap the one that is true for you.

Learn

  • Honest. Consistent with your CV. Easy to back up with an example.
  • No work experience? Use university, sport, clubs or volunteering.
  • What you claim here can come back in the final interview. Tip

Seen before

  • Candidates call this part questionnaires about you and your experience.Reported · 2024–2026

Part D · about 55 statements · 5–10 min (estimate)

Your work style

What it is

Many short statements about how you like to work. Often you see two statements and pick the one that is more like you. Unconfirmed

Why they use it

They compare your work style with people who do well in the role.

Try it

Which statement is more like you?

No right answer. Tap the one that sounds like you.

Learn

  • Be your best honest work self.
  • The same idea comes back in other words. Answer it the same way.
  • Keep a steady pace. Do not stop long on one item.
  • Keep the real job in mind: commercial, team-based and customer-facing.

Maybe · a few items

Short number questions

What it is

Two candidates in 2025 mentioned number, verbal or logic questions. Most reports do not. It is not confirmed for this round. Reported

Try it

Sales grow from €80k to €92k. What is the growth in percent?

15%. (92 − 80) ÷ 80 = 0.15.

A €50 product gets 20% off, then another 10% off. What is the final price?

€36. 50 × 0.8 = 40, then 40 × 0.9 = 36. Discounts multiply. They do not add up to 30%.

Learn

One hour with percentages and simple charts is cheap insurance. Tip

Station 3 of 3

Video interview

About 10–15 minutes · usually 3 questions · camera on

What it is

A one-way recorded interview. A short video of a young employee asks each question. You think, then you record your answer. Nobody is live on the other side. Official

How it runs

  1. PracticeAs many practice questions as you like. They do not see these.
  2. ThinkA countdown. You can start early.
  3. Record3, 2, 1, then the timer runs.
  4. DoneTap Done Answering, or it stops at the limit.
  5. SubmitAt the very end. Then you are finished.

The settings

Prepare for 30 seconds

Most likely 2 to 3 minutes
Prepare for 1:30 to 2:00

Most likely between questions

Why they use it

To hear real examples of C, A and R in your own words, and your motivation. Recruiters care more about what you say than about how relaxed you look on camera.

Try it: practice timer

Asked before · reported

Tell us about a time you had a different opinion from someone. What did you do?

Thinking time
Answer time

Ready

0:30

Light band = the sweet spot, 1:30 to 2:00.

Press Start. Think first, then answer out loud. You can film yourself on your phone at the same time.

Learn: STAR in about two minutes

S + T≈ 20 s Action≈ 60 s Result≈ 20–30 s
  • Say I. They need to hear your part in the team. Official
  • End with the result and one thing you learned.
  • Three keywords on paper. No script.

Worked example: a time you had a different opinion from someone.

S + T
In a four-person university project, a teammate wanted to survey 500 people. I thought 50 good interviews would teach us more in the time we had.
Action
I asked why he preferred the survey. I suggested a one-week test: ten interviews and a 50-person pilot survey. I shared both results with the group, and we combined the methods. CR
Result
We delivered on time and got the highest grade in the class. I learned to test a disagreement with data instead of arguing about it. A

Bring your sales side

Past candidates do not report sales-only questions. You can still show sales strengths inside the normal questions: a no that you turned around, someone you persuaded without being their boss, a target you set and hit, a customer you kept happy, a choice where you used numbers.

The company says it wants to be the most customer-focused energy marketer and trader. If that is true for you too, your answer to why you want to join can connect to it. Official

Behind the screen

How you are scored

  1. If a system scores your video, it reads your words. HireVue says it turns your speech into text and scores that. It says it does not score your face, body language, background or tone. Official

  2. A recruiter reviews your answers too. Official

  3. The job tryout gives a fit score per skill. Employers can let the system move people forward above a set score. The settings for this programme are not public. Official

  4. If AI scoring is used, a consent screen should tell you at the start. HireVue's design lets you opt out and be rated by a person with the same scale. Official

  5. Many candidates get a short strengths report after the tryout. Reported

MythThe AI reads my face and eye contact.
FactHireVue stopped facial analysis in 2021. In the EU, emotion-reading AI is banned in hiring.
MythI should fill my answers with keywords.
FactHireVue says it scores the whole answer in context.
MythI can pause in the middle of an answer.
FactRecording runs until you stop it or time runs out. You can take a break between questions.
MythThey see my practice answers.
FactPractice recordings are not saved. Only you see them.
MythRetakes are always unlimited.
FactThe employer sets the number. The attempts screen shows it before you start.

A system may read first. People decide. Clear, specific and honest answers work for both.

Fine print, made short

Rules and your rights

The rules

  1. 7 days. The invitation usually arrives within 24 hours of applying. From then on you have 7 days. Official

  2. Missed the 7 days? Your application is withdrawn. You can apply again after 10 months. Official

  3. Not successful? You can apply again after 10 months. Your result counts for that long. Official

  4. No AI tools during the test. The company does not allow it in the tryout or the video. The platform can notice when you leave the screen, and it blocks pasting. Official

  5. Click Submit at the very end. That tells the recruiter you are done. Official

Your rights

  • Extra timeIf you need it, request it on the first screen of the interview, or ask the graduate recruitment team before you start.
  • No emotion-reading AIBanned in EU hiring since February 2025.
  • Told when it is AIIf an AI talks to you, it must say so. This applies since August 2026.
  • Reasons and a humanYou can ask why you were rejected, whether the decision was automated, and for a human review.
  • Dutch recruitment codeReasons within two weeks, and your data deleted within four weeks unless you agree to keep it. The code is voluntary.

General information, not legal advice.

Three short days

A simple plan

  1. Day 1

    Stories

    Write keywords for the six stories. Draft your answer to why you want to join, 60 to 90 seconds long.

  2. Day 2

    Say it out loud

    Use the practice timer on six to eight questions. Film yourself on your phone and watch two answers back. Fix the length, and say I more often. Try the work-situation examples once more.

  3. Day 3

    Calm run

    Do the setup checklist on the real device. Optional: 30 minutes of percentages. Then take the assessment on a day you feel sharp, and not on the last day.

Invitation already here? You have 7 days. One to three days of preparation is enough. Tip

Easy to avoid

Ten common mistakes

  1. Waiting until the last day.A tech problem then costs you the deadline, and 10 months.

  2. Starting before your stories are ready.Your result counts for 10 months.

  3. Skipping the practice question.It is free, and nobody sees it.

  4. Saying we and never I.They need to hear your part.

  5. A general answer to why this company.Name the programme and one real reason.

  6. No result at the end.Finish with the outcome and what you learned.

  7. Reading a script.It sounds flat. Use three keywords.

  8. Re-recording until the energy is gone.Keep take one or two.

  9. A noisy room or weak Wi-Fi.A dropped connection can submit half an answer.

  10. Using AI tools during the test.Not allowed, and the platform can notice.
